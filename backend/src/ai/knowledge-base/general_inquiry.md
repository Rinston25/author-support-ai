# General Inquiries

Use the company overview for questions about packages (Standard Free and Bestseller Breakthrough) and what BookLeaf handles (cover design, typesetting, ISBN assignment, printing, distribution, and royalty management).

## Guidance from sample responses

- Updating the description of a book on Amazon after it is live: yes, metadata updates can be submitted through the dashboard or by emailing the BookLeaf team. Changes typically reflect on platforms within 3-5 business days.
- Royalty payouts are made via bank transfer to the account linked in the author's dashboard.
