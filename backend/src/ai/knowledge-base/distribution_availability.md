# Distribution & Availability

- Books are listed on Amazon India, Flipkart, Amazon US, Amazon UK, and the BookLeaf Store.
- New listings typically go live within 7-10 business days after publication is complete.
- If a book is showing as unavailable on a platform, it usually indicates a stock sync issue. BookLeaf's team can trigger a re-sync within 24-48 hours.

## Guidance from sample responses

- Book shows "Currently Unavailable" on Amazon: explain that this is typically a stock sync issue. Confirm you are triggering a re-sync with the distribution team. Set expectation: 24-48 hours for it to go live again.
