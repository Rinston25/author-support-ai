# Sample Query -> Response Examples

Each example is tagged with the category key it belongs to. Use these to calibrate tone and content.

## Example
category: royalty_payments
Author query: I published my book 4 months ago and still haven't received any royalty. What's going on?
Expected response: Acknowledge the frustration. Explain the quarterly royalty cycle and 45-day payout window. Check if their bank details are linked. Provide the specific next payout date. If the payment is genuinely overdue, escalate with a 48-hour resolution timeline.

## Example
category: royalty_payments
Author query: My royalty amount seems too low. I sold 200 copies but only received 3,000.
Expected response: Explain the net profit calculation: MRP minus printing, platform commission, and shipping. Offer to share a detailed line-by-line royalty breakdown. Don't be defensive; offer transparency.

## Example
category: isbn_metadata
Author query: My book is showing a different ISBN on Amazon than what's on the physical copy.
Expected response: Treat as high priority. Acknowledge this is a serious data issue. Confirm you are escalating to the production team immediately. Provide a 48-hour resolution timeline.

## Example
category: printing_quality
Author query: I received my author copies and the print quality is terrible. The images are blurry and pages are misaligned.
Expected response: Apologise sincerely. Ask the author to share photos of the defective copies. Confirm that BookLeaf will arrange a free reprint once verified. Provide a timeline of 5-7 business days for the reprint.

## Example
category: distribution_availability
Author query: My book is published but it's showing as "Currently Unavailable" on Amazon.
Expected response: Explain that this is typically a stock sync issue. Confirm you're triggering a re-sync with the distribution team. Set expectation: 24-48 hours for it to go live again.

## Example
category: book_status_production
Author query: It's been 3 weeks and my book is still in typesetting. When will it be done?
Expected response: Check the actual production status. If it's delayed, be honest about why (e.g., waiting on author approval for proofs). Provide a specific updated timeline. Don't blame the author even if the delay is on their side; frame it collaboratively.

## Example
category: general_inquiry
Author query: Can I update the description of my book on Amazon after it's already live?
Expected response: Yes, metadata updates can be submitted through the dashboard or by emailing the BookLeaf team. Changes typically reflect on platforms within 3-5 business days.
